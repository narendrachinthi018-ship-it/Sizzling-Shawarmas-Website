---
name: Restaurant content integrity
description: Content rules for restaurant ordering experiences when owner-approved pricing or review copy is unavailable.
---

Menu pricing and customer review copy must stay explicitly pending until supplied or verified by the restaurant owner; never replace missing business data with plausible invented values.

**Why:** The ordering site needs to be trustworthy, and estimated menu prices or fabricated testimonials can mislead customers.

**How to apply:** Show a clear pending state in menu/cart totals and reserve review highlights for owner-supplied or verified listing content.

Google Maps may expose only a stable thumbnail while keeping the complete restaurant photo/video set inside its interactive listing viewer.

**Why:** Directly embedding unstable viewer URLs can break or show stale media, while the live listing keeps the owner/customer media current.

**How to apply:** Use any stable listing photo as a sourced local asset, and link the full photo/video gallery back to the supplied Google Maps listing.
